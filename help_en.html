COLONY

A strategy game about two ant colonies: gather food and wood, raise an army, research technologies, survive among the wildlife — and defeat your neighbours.

About and goal

Colony is an economic strategy game about two ant colonies: yours and a computer opponent. The enemy anthill stands mirrored across the centre of the field. Your task is to gather resources wisely, grow your colony and break your rival.

Victory and defeat

  • Victory — the enemy has no workers left (eggs in the incubator included) and less than 5 food, so it cannot hatch a new worker.
  • Defeat — the same has happened to your colony.
  • Buildings cannot be destroyed: the colonies only kill each other's ants.

Starting a game

The main menu has two ways to start:

  • New Game — the usual start: 25 food and 10 wood.
  • A Lot Of Resources Mode — both sides start with 1500 food and 500 wood: you can build, research and fight right away.

In A Lot Of Resources Mode, while you still have food (at least 10), there is an arms race: as soon as you buy more warriors than the enemy has, it hatches warriors of the same total strength (Mighty Warriors included) and buys no upgrades meanwhile. While the strength is equal, it spends on upgrades and warehouses only what it has above your stores. So the fastest victory cannot be won by one early rush.

While a game is on, the menu also shows Continue and Restart (the same map from the beginning). There are also Statistics — your counters over all games — and the Steam Leaderboards of the fastest victories.

Then the game setup screen opens; the game begins with the Start button. Your choices are remembered.

Difficulty

Difficulty changes only the computer opponent:

LevelWhat changes
ExplorerThe enemy builds no warriors, sets no traps and never attacks — explore the field and the economy in peace. No achievements are given in this mode.
EasyThe enemy gathers 30% less, sets no traps and attacks no earlier than after 6 minutes.
NormalThe enemy gathers 10% less and attacks no earlier than after 3 minutes.
HardThe enemy plays at full strength from the very start.

Map and view

  • Map: Small (800×600), Medium (1152×864), Large (1440×1080), Huge (1728×1296). A larger map has proportionally more stones, swamps and grass.
  • Fog of war — you see only what is near your anthill, warehouses and ants. Explored ground stays half-visible; a found enemy anthill and warehouses stay on the map. Explosions and effects in the fog are hidden. Every level of Genetic boost increases the sight range.
  • Whole map visible — the mouse wheel can zoom out to the whole field. Without it you cannot zoom out beyond 800×600 — the field scrolls.

World settings

Every element of the surroundings and the wildlife can be turned on or off, and its amount chosen (Few / Normal / Many): stones, swamps, snags, ponds, the stream, mushrooms, water striders, snails (only with ponds or the stream), ground beetles, spiders, the mantis, red ants. "Reset to Default" brings back the standard world. Bottom right — a live preview of the map.

Choosing the anthill's place

After Start the camera flies over the field, then pick the place for your colony with a click. Hexagons mark the allowed places; the anthill cannot stand too close to the centre or the edges. The enemy appears mirrored.

The first thing to do is to hatch at least one worker (a hint at the bottom of the screen).

Interface

  • Build — hatching ants and buildings.
  • Technology — upgrades, automation and traps (after the laboratory is built).
  • food and wood — your stores.
  • Area — drawing gathering areas: On — add, Off; keys = / -.
  • Attack — the orders and attack panel. Mode — forestry, mushroom farming and unit behavior.
  • On the right — the game time. At the bottom — the hint line and the panel of selected ants.

Camera

  • Mouse wheel — zoom in/out towards the cursor (up to 2.5×).
  • Right button held — drag the field ("grab and pull"); a short right click still clears the selection.
  • Arrow keys — scroll the field; the right (or middle) mouse button held — drag the field.
  • Home — to your anthill.

Hover hints

Point at an ant, a plant, a mushroom, a trap, a creature or an anthill — a card shows up: health, age, growth, ripening, rotting, the trap's charging, a red ants' nest's stores and so on.

Resources

Food

Grows from seeds: when a group of plants ripens, it gives new seeds. Gathered by workers. Food left ungathered for long turns woody and becomes wood.

Wood

Gathered by architects. Comes from food left ungathered for long and grows as reeds on pond shores. Needed for the laboratory, warehouses, warriors and some technologies.

Mushrooms

One more source of food after the New resource type technology. Details — in Mushrooms.

Gathering and delivery

  • Ants gather resources only inside gathering areas (or by a direct order) and carry them to the nearest warehouse or anthill.
  • With a load an ant walks 30% slower.
  • Idle ants with nothing to gather blink as spots on the anthill.

Ant trails

Where the colony's ants walk often, the grass is trodden into a grainy sandy trail. On their own trail ants run faster — up to +15%. An abandoned trail grows over in a few minutes. Enemy trails are seen only where you see the field.

Place warehouses so that the way "field → warehouse" is short and well trodden: the trail itself will speed up the delivery.

Units

Ants are hatched on the Build panel: first an egg appears in the incubator around the anthill (up to 16 at once), then an ant hatches from it. Shift + key orders 16 ants at once.

UnitCostRole
Worker E5Gathers food and mushrooms. 1 health point.
Architect A3 2Gathers wood and reeds. 1 health point. In a New Game neither you nor the enemy can hatch architects during the first 30 seconds after the game starts (the first worker), so that the match begins fairly; in A Lot Of Resources Mode — right away.
Warrior W35 10*Attack and defence. Health 10 (15/20/25 with Mighty Warriors). Needs the laboratory.

* With every level of Mighty Warriors a warrior costs more: 35/15, 45/25, 50/30. The upgrade affects only new warriors.

Limit and age

  • No more than 40 workers and no more than 40 architects; every warehouse adds +10 to each limit. Warriors are not limited.
  • Ants grow old and die of old age — the age bar in the hint goes from green to red. In a swamp they age 10 times faster.
  • In an attack ants run 30% faster than usual.

Workers and architects bite too

By order, workers and architects can be sent to attack enemy ants and creatures. They bite weakly (creatures — four times weaker than a warrior), and their bite is not upgraded — a last resort when there are no warriors.

Buildings and traps

Anthill

The main building: placed before the game starts. Resources are brought here, ants are hatched here.

Laboratory L

An annex to the anthill, no place to choose. Unlocks warriors, technologies and traps.

Cost: 35

Warehouse S

A close delivery point for resources and +10 to the limit of workers and architects. Up to 5 warehouses; More warehouses adds more.

Cost: 25

Trap B

A mine: explodes when an enemy ant or a creature steps on it.

Cost: 1 1

Where a warehouse can stand

  • Only within reach of the anthill or another of your warehouses — the network of warehouses grows step by step (the reach is shown with dashed circles).
  • Not too close to growing resources.
  • Not on snags, mushrooms, ponds and reeds, the stream, red ants' nests, traps, food, wood or webs. By a pond's shore — fine.
  • The enemy follows the same rules.

If a warehouse, a trap or an area cannot be placed somewhere, a hint with the reason appears by the cursor: "Too close to an anthill", "Only inside your zones", "Nothing to gather here" and so on.

Traps

  • Placed only inside your gathering areas on your half of the field — while you choose the place, the areas are shown on the field.
  • After placing, a trap charges (about 17 seconds, the progress is shown in the hint), then becomes active.
  • Goes off on enemy ants, wild creatures and red ants. The blast kills ants within its radius, a spider, a ground beetle, a water strider; it takes 60 health from a mantis.
  • Enemy traps are dangerous too. The enemy sets traps from Normal difficulty up.
  • The Traps automation places them by itself.

Mode and gathering areas

Gathering areas

Press Area (or =) and draw areas on the field — ants gather inside them. - — remove areas. If resources are far from a warehouse, better not put an area there until a warehouse stands nearby.

The Mode panel

  • Forestry F — mark the plants the workers leave alone: they will grow into wood. "+" by the cursor — mark, "−" — unmark.
  • Mushroom farming G — the same for mushrooms the workers do not gather.
  • Unit behavior:
    • Attack everyone — idle warriors protect the workers: they rush at any creature or enemy that attacked them.
    • Harvesting — all efforts go to gathering resources.

Orders and selection

Selection

  • Box: hold the left button on the field and drag a green box — the ants inside are selected (warriors if any, otherwise architects, otherwise workers).
  • Tab — select all your warriors.
  • The selected ones are shown bottom left: a tile per ant with a health bar, their number and total strength.
  • Right button — clear the selection and close open panels.
  • Selected ants obey only you: colony defence, Attack everyone and the auto attack do not take them.

Where you can click

The field

The ants go to the point (a running dashed line) and wait there.

Outpost

A second click next to the same point, once the warriors are there, makes an outpost: the warriors hold the place, strike enemies that come near and return. A flag stands over the point and the dashed ring "breathes". Only a new order to these warriors ends the outpost. Before the second click a hint flag keeps falling onto the point.

Food / a mushroom

Workers gather food near that place, even outside the areas, and carry it home. A mushroom — they gnaw it (if mushrooms are researched).

Wood

Architects gather wood near that place.

An enemy / a creature

The selected ants attack the target (a red dashed circle). When it is killed they switch to the young by the nest, otherwise they wait.

The green sight by the cursor shows where ants can be sent. Once given an order, the ants carry it out even after the selection is cleared, and then go back to their usual work. Attacks ordered by clicking an enemy are shown on the result charts too.

Attack

The Attack panel:

  • At the top — the type of your ants (a click switches it), below — the type of enemy ants to attack.
  • "‹ 1 ›" — how many ants to send (hold — fast). The chosen ones are marked with a ring.
  • Attack — send the squad.
  • Type attack: Direct — head on; Maneuver — round in an arc (needs the Maneuver technology).
  • Constant attack — warriors are sent at the enemy all the time; the panel stays open and shows how many are "in attack".

Combat

  • Health is measured in the same units for everyone: a worker and an architect — 1, a warrior — 10…25.
  • A warrior's health is shown by the petals around it: your warriors' petals are red-orange, the enemy warriors' are blue.
  • When the Attack panel is closed, the ants that waited for orders go back to work.
  • If the colony is attacked, an alarm sounds and the music changes to battle music.

There are one to four ponds, and both sides get them equally: one in the middle, pairs mirrored. If the map has a stream, offshoots run from it to the ponds near it — narrow channels flowing into the pond (mirrored on both halves). A channel, like the stream, cannot be crossed: it is walked round the pond.

Mushrooms

Mycelia grow by snags and especially willingly by swamps. Mushrooms can be gathered after the New resource type technology.

Parasol mushroom

The biggest: up to 13 food.

Bolete

Big: up to 11 food.

Fly agaric

Medium: up to 6 food.

Chanterelle

Small: up to 4 food.

  • The bigger the mushroom, the more food, but the longer it takes to gnaw. Workers take only ripe mushrooms; young ones keep growing and feed the mycelium.
  • A progress circle is shown over the mushroom; a gnawed piece is carried home. An ungathered mushroom rots and disappears in time.
  • A mycelium sends out threads; where the threads of two mycelia meet, a new one appears. A thread that reaches a swamp drinks from it — such a mycelium gives mushrooms 1.7 times more often, and the ones linked to it 1.35 times.
  • The longer the grown-together network of mycelium, the more often mushrooms appear (up to 2.5×) and the bigger they are.
  • If more than half of a mycelium is eaten, new mushrooms appear many times slower; one eaten completely grows back very slowly.
Turn on Mushroom farming: the mushrooms left keep the mycelium at full strength, and it feeds you longer.

Wildlife

Creatures belong neither to you nor to the enemy and live far from both anthills. If Attack everyone is chosen, idle warriors run by themselves at a creature that attacked your workers. An enemy with enough warriors plans a hunt for creatures in its way.

Garden spider

health 30bite to a warrior −4spiderlings: 12 / −2, up to 6

Weaves a web by the stones and waits in the middle; rushes at any ant that comes near and carries the prey off in a cocoon. Kills a worker or an architect with one bite. Spiderlings hatch from the cocoons (1 warrior + 1 worker or 3 workers). The last spider, badly hurt, runs to other stones and weaves a new web. If a spider is attacked, the whole family rushes at the warriors. A spider cut off by the stream looks for a way over a crossing.

Ground beetle

health 40bite to a warrior −4young: 14 / −2, up to 4

Lives in a burrow under a snag and hunts lone ants (it leaves groups of three or more alone). Faster than a usual ant — you can run away only after the second Genetic boost. A strong shell: warriors hurt it slower. Wounded, it hides in its burrow and heals; without young it may run to an empty snag and dig a new burrow. It does not chase prey across the stream.

Snail

health 10–50does not attackreward 10–70 food

Now and then crawls out of a pond or the stream and slowly crawls about the field (going round stones and hills, leaving a shiny trail) — mostly by the water, but sometimes, "tired", it crawls far away. The longer it crawls, the bigger it grows: 6 stages; it comes out of the water at one of the first three. Crawling over food, it eats it — up to 10 pieces, then it rests. Workers and architects gnaw a snail (send them at it, like at a mushroom); warriors do not hurt it. The bigger the snail, the more food it gives — how much is shown in the hint; the food goes to the side whose ants are by it in greater number. In the World settings: Snails — Few / Normal / Many (only with ponds or the stream).

Praying mantis — the boss

health 150strike to a warrior −8reward 60 food

How many there are is set in the World settings: Few — one, Normal — two (one on a small map), Many — two to four. It lies in ambush in the neutral strip, almost blending with the grass. It strikes any ant within reach of its forelegs like lightning: a worker dies, a warrior loses 8 health. It fights back against everyone around — you need a crowd of warriors. The side with more warriors at the body when it dies gets 60 food. A trap takes 60 of its health. A killed mantis does not appear again.

Water strider

health 20knocks the load out

Glides over the pond and knocks food or wood out of an ant walking right along the shore — the load sinks. It never swims up where a warrior is near (closer than ~70), so a patrol area by the pond protects the shore. A killed water strider is replaced by a new one in time.

Red ants

ant 12nest 250neutral

A neutral colony: one nest in the centre (Few), two mirrored (Normal) or two on each half (Many). The red ants gather crumbs around their home and touch no one until they are touched.

  • Warriors that come right up to the nest are simply driven away — no war.
  • The side whose warriors attacked a red ant or a nest gets a feud: its ants by the nest are attacked, and from time to time a red squad goes on a raid on its workers.
  • A destroyed nest gives all its food stores to the side with more warriors nearby. The stores are shown in the hint.
  • No food or mushrooms grow by red ants' nests; patrols go round them.

New colonies

Having stored 200 food, a nest gives 75 of it and half of its ants to a new colony: the squad goes to a free place on the same bank (as when the map is made — not by water, stones, mushrooms, food, warehouses, traps, other red nests or your colonies; if the place gets spoilt on the way, another one is chosen). If more than half of the squad is killed on the way, the rest run home. On the spot the ants carry twigs, and the nest grows in 8 stages (the hint shows "Building: N%"); the building can be destroyed. A finished one is an ordinary red ants' nest with its own stores. In all there are no more than 3 / 4 / 5 / 7 red nests on the map — from a small to a huge map, but always at least two more than at the start.

The enemy

The computer opponent plays by the same rules: hatches ants, builds a laboratory and a chain of warehouses, sets areas (red dashes), researches technologies, sets traps, attacks and crosses the stream by the crossings.

  • Hunts creatures in its way when it has enough warriors.
  • Protects its workers in the aggressive mode.
  • Its behaviour depends on the chosen difficulty.

Results and charts

After a victory or a defeat the results screen opens: the game time, the difficulty and map size, the Restart and To menu buttons. After the victory/defeat tune the main menu music plays again.

  • Summary — resources earned, ants destroyed and lost by type, researched technologies, traps (placed / destroyed / by enemy), wildlife slain by the colonies.
  • Charts — the number of ants and the resources of both sides over time. Swords mark attacks: the number on the icon is how many different ants took part (attacks ordered by clicking an enemy are counted too).

Statistics and leaderboards

  • Statistics (main menu) — counters over all games: games played (a game counts after 2 minutes of play or if it ends sooner), victories and defeats by difficulty, time played, resources gathered, ants destroyed and lost, warehouses and traps, creatures defeated. The counters are kept next to the game and in Steam.
  • Leaderboards — the fastest victory for every difficulty, map size and mode (normal / rich): the world top and your Steam friends. After a victory the results screen shows your place. Explorer results are not recorded.

Settings

  • Sound and Music — on/off and volume bars (10% steps).
  • Scroll speed — how fast the field scrolls at the screen edge and with the arrows: 50% — normal, 100% — twice as fast, 0% — the field does not scroll at the edge (only by dragging).
  • Fullscreen — On (default): the game fills the screen; Off: in a window. Also switched with Alt+Enter.
  • Resolution — for the fullscreen mode, a drop-down list up to 4K (by default — the desktop resolution).
  • Dynamic menu background — On: a video plays in the main menu; Off (default): the still first frame is shown.
  • Language — 9 languages: Russian, English, Ukrainian, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Polish, Turkish.
  • Hotkeys — any key can be reassigned; "Reset to Default" brings back the standard ones.

The music follows the situation: menu, peaceful play, battle, victory, defeat.

Tips

  • Start with a few workers and, after 30 seconds, a couple of architects: 35 wood for the laboratory and 25 for a warehouse are the main early milestone.
  • Place warehouses by resource-rich spots far from the anthill — the delivery gets short and the trails speed the ants up.
  • Turn on Attack everyone and keep a couple of warriors by the gathering areas — they protect the workers from spiders, ground beetles and water striders.
  • Do not walk workers through swamps: there they are slow and age fast.
  • Do not touch the red ants without need — a feud brings raids on your workers. But a destroyed nest gives all its food stores.
  • Go at the mantis with a big crowd or lead it onto traps — 60 food is worth it.
  • Put warriors on an outpost by a gathering area (a second click on the same point) — they will not wander off and will protect the workers.
  • Send workers to gnaw a big snail crawling over your food — it gives up to 70 food.
  • Inventory increase works as a percentage of your stores — it pays more when you have many resources.
